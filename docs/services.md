## Why these offerings, given the SKILL is open source

The SKILL is open source under MIT for non-commercial use. The commercial offerings exist because:

1. **Commercial use of the framework in paid services requires a commercial license** (per `LICENSE`).
2. **Methodology depth matters at high stakes.** A self-installed SKILL can produce a verdict; an experienced practitioner can interpret edge cases, customize profiles for specific domains, and design appropriate organizational integration. That distinction matters most when the decisions being audited are board-level, regulatory, or financial.
3. **Reference architecture without expert support is rarely deployed correctly at enterprise scale.** Workshops and certification ensure the framework is applied as designed.
4. **Custom audit engagements provide an external attestation** that some buyers (regulators, board chairs, due-diligence committees) require — the same way auditors, appraisers, and surveyors do in their respective domains.

The open-source SKILL is the foundation. The commercial offerings are how the framework scales into the contexts where its design matters most.
